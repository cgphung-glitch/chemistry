Problem Solving & Chemistry Rules:

  • Rule 1: Compound molar masses are always provided directly in the problem prompt.
  • Rule 2: Water ($\ce{H2O}$) is never selected as a target or given species in calculations.
  • Rule 3: Acids are treated strictly as aqueous solutions ($M$, $mL$) or titration titrants and are never measured directly by mass (grams).
  • Rule 4: Gases and pure metals are excluded from solution volume and concentration calculations.
