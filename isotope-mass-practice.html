Formula & Concept Reference

1. Average Atomic Mass Formula

\[ \text{Avg Mass} = \sum \left(\text{Isotope Mass}_i \times \frac{\%\text{ Abundance}_i}{100}\right) \]

Multiply each isotope's exact mass by its decimal abundance (percentage divided by 100) and sum all products together.

2. Atom Counts to Percent Abundance

\[ \%\text{ Abundance}_i = \left(\frac{N_i}{N_{\text{total}}}\right) \times 100\% \]

In mass spectrometry count samples, divide the number of atoms of isotope \(i\) by the total atom count in the sample, then multiply by 100%.
