📖 Gas Law Formulas

Boyle's Law (constant T, n)

$P_1 V_1 = P_2 V_2$

Charles' Law (constant P, n)

$\dfrac{V_1}{T_1} = \dfrac{V_2}{T_2}$

Gay-Lussac's Law (constant V, n)

$\dfrac{P_1}{T_1} = \dfrac{P_2}{T_2}$

Avogadro's Law (constant P, T)

$\dfrac{V_1}{n_1} = \dfrac{V_2}{n_2}$

Combined Gas Law (constant n)

$\dfrac{P_1 V_1}{T_1} = \dfrac{P_2 V_2}{T_2}$

Ideal Gas Law

$PV = nRT$
$R = 0.0821\ \dfrac{\text{L}\cdot\text{atm}}{\text{mol}\cdot\text{K}}$

Dalton's Law of Partial Pressures

$P_{\text{total}} = P_1 + P_2 + P_3 + \cdots$

Gas Density

$d = \dfrac{PM}{RT}$

M = molar mass (g/mol); d in g/L

RMS Speed (Kinetic Molecular Theory)

$v_{\text{rms}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{3RT}{M}}$

Use R = 8.314 J/(mol·K) and M in kg/mol for v_rms in m/s

Graham's Law of Effusion

$\dfrac{\text{rate}_1}{\text{rate}_2} = \sqrt{\dfrac{M_2}{M_1}}$

Lighter gases (smaller M) effuse faster

Temperature Conversion

$T(\text{K}) = T(^\circ\text{C}) + 273.15$
