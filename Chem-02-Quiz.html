Chemistry Cheat Sheet & Reference

Topic 1: Subatomic Particles Formulae

  • Protons: \(p = Z\) (Atomic Number)
  • Neutrons: \(n = A - Z\) (Mass Number minus Atomic Number)
  • Electrons: \(e^- = p - C\) (Protons minus Charge)
  • Example: For \(^{35}_{17}\text{Cl}^-\): \(p = 17\), \(n = 35 - 17 = 18\), \(e^- = 17 - (-1) = 18\).

Topic 2: Periodic Groups

Alkali Metals (Grp 1): Li, Na, K, Rb, Cs, Fr
Alkaline Earth (Grp 2): Be, Mg, Ca, Sr, Ba, Ra
Pnictogens (Grp 15): N, P, As, Sb, Bi
Chalcogens (Grp 16): O, S, Se, Te, Po
Halogens (Grp 17): F, Cl, Br, I, At
Noble Gases (Grp 18): He, Ne, Ar, Kr, Xe, Rn

Topic 3: Compound Types

  • Molecular (Covalent): Nonmetal + Nonmetal (e.g., \(\text{CO}_2\), \(\text{H}_2\text{O}\), \(\text{NH}_3\)).
  • Ionic: Metal + Nonmetal or Polyatomic Ions (e.g., \(\text{NaCl}\), \(\text{MgCl}_2\), \(\text{KNO}_3\)).
  • Acid: Yields \(\text{H}^+\) in solution, usually starts with H (e.g., \(\text{HCl}\), \(\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4\), \(\text{HNO}_3\)).
  • Base: Yields \(\text{OH}^-\) or accepts \(\text{H}^+\) (e.g., \(\text{NaOH}\), \(\text{KOH}\), \(\text{NH}_3\)).
  • Hydrate: Ionic crystal with bound water molecules (e.g., \(\text{CuSO}_4 \cdot 5\text{H}_2\text{O}\)).

Topic 4: Key Atomic Discoveries

  • John Dalton: Atomic Theory, Solid Sphere model, Fixed mass ratios.
  • J.J. Thomson: Cathode Ray Tube, Discovered Electrons, Plum Pudding Model.
  • Robert Millikan: Oil Drop Experiment, Calculated Charge of Electron (\(1.602 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}\)).
  • Ernest Rutherford: Gold Foil Experiment, Dense positively charged Nucleus.
  • James Chadwick: Discovered the neutral Neutron in the nucleus.
