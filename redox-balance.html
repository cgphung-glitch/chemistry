How to Balance Redox Reactions

Half-Reaction Method Steps

  1. Divide: Separate the overall reaction into oxidation and reduction half-reactions.
  2. Balance Elements: Balance all elements except Oxygen and Hydrogen first.
  3. Balance Oxygen: Add H2O molecules to the oxygen-deficient side.
  4. Balance Hydrogen:
    • Acidic Medium: Add H+ ions to balance H.
    • Basic Medium: Add H2O to the H-deficient side and equal OH- to the opposite side.
    • Neutral Medium: Use H2O as needed.
  5. Balance Charge: Add electrons (e-) to the more positive side.
  6. Equalize & Combine: Multiply reactions so lost e- equal gained e-, then sum and simplify.
