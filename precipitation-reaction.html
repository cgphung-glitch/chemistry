How to Enter Chemical Formulas & Equations

1. Charge Syntax (^ Required)

All charges MUST immediately follow a caret ^.

  • Ba^2+(aq) or Ba^+2(aq) → Ba2+(aq)
  • NO3^-(aq) → NO3-(aq)
  • SO4^2-(aq) → SO42-(aq)

2. Coefficients and Order

• Steps 1 & 4: No coefficients are needed. Just list species separated by + or ,.

• Steps 2, 3, & 5: Include stoichiometric coefficients (omit if 1). Write reactants on the left side and products on the right side using =, ->, or -->.

• Order does NOT matter! You can list species on either side of the equation in any order.

3. States of Matter

Include physical states in parentheses: (aq) for aqueous and (s) for solid precipitate.
